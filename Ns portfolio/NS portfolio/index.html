About

I started writing code to fix something specific: my family kept missing government benefits they qualified for, because nobody could parse the paperwork.

That problem became SchemeLab, and SchemeLab became the reason I now spend most nights in a terminal. One year in, I've gone from printing hello world to shipping full-stack apps with real auth, real databases, and real users who tell me when something breaks.

I care about interfaces that feel fast and honest, code the next person can read, and problems where the solution is measurably better than what came before. Currently looking for an internship where I can learn from engineers better than me.

FLAGSHIP · FULL-STACK

SchemeLab

An eligibility engine for Indian government schemes. Answer eight plain questions — age, income, state, occupation — and it returns the schemes you actually qualify for, the documents you'll need, and the office you apply at. Rules live in a versioned JSON ruleset, so a policy change is a data edit, not a redeploy.
